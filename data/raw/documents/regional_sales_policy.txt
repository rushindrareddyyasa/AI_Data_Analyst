REGIONAL SALES PERFORMANCE POLICY

Purpose
-------
This policy defines how regional sales performance is evaluated.

Quarterly Sales Targets
-----------------------
Each region is assigned a quarterly sales target.

A region is considered high-performing when its quarterly sales
exceed its assigned target by more than 10 percent.

A region that achieves between 90 percent and 110 percent of its
target is considered to be performing within the expected range.

A region achieving less than 90 percent of its target requires
performance review.

Regional Performance Review
---------------------------
Regional performance is evaluated using total sales generated
during the relevant quarter.

Managers should review sales performance together with profit,
discount levels, and customer-related metrics before making
business decisions.

Discount Policy
---------------
Discounts should be monitored because excessive discounting can
increase sales while reducing profitability.

A high-sales region should therefore not automatically be
considered the most profitable region.

Reporting
---------
Regional sales performance should be reviewed regularly to identify
strong-performing regions, underperforming regions, and changes in
business performance over time.
